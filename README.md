# 📊 Apex Retail | Monthly Sales Report

### 📈 Power BI Dashboard | Sales Analytics | Business Intelligence

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Apex Retail Monthly Sales Report** is a Power BI dashboard designed to analyze and visualize retail sales performance over a six-month period, from **May through October**.

The dashboard provides valuable insights into monthly revenue trends, regional sales performance, and product category demand.

By transforming sales data into clear and meaningful visualizations, this project helps identify business trends, compare regional performance, and understand customer purchasing patterns.

This project demonstrates my ability to create business intelligence reports and communicate data-driven insights using **Microsoft Power BI**.

## 📊 Dashboard Features & Visualizations

### 💰 1. Monthly Revenue Analysis

**Visualization:** Line Chart

This chart displays total monthly sales revenue from May through October.

| 📅 Month | 💵 Revenue |
|---|---:|
| May | $10.8K |
| June | $13.1K |
| July | $14.8K |
| August | $18.4K |
| September | $17.8K |
| October | $14.0K |

**🔍 Key Insights:**

- 📈 Revenue increased consistently from May through August.
- 🏆 **August recorded the highest revenue at approximately $18.4K.**
- September maintained strong performance at approximately $17.8K.
- 📉 Revenue declined to $14.0K in October.
- May recorded the lowest monthly revenue at approximately $10.8K.

**💡 Business Insight:**

The upward revenue trend from May to August indicates strong sales growth during this period. The decline in September and October suggests an opportunity to investigate seasonal changes, customer demand, or other factors affecting sales performance.

### 🌎 2. Monthly Total Revenue by Region

**Visualization:** Multi-Series Line Chart

This chart compares monthly revenue across four geographical regions:

- 🔵 Midwest
- 🔷 Northeast
- 🟠 Southeast
- 🟣 West

**🔍 Key Insights:**

- 🏆 The Northeast region demonstrates strong revenue performance across multiple months.
- The Northeast reaches its highest monthly revenue in September, at approximately $8K.
- The West experiences a significant revenue increase in August.
- The Midwest maintains relatively stable revenue throughout the reporting period.
- The Southeast shows moderate fluctuations in monthly revenue.

**💡 Business Insight:**

Regional comparisons help identify the strongest sales markets and highlight opportunities for improvement in regions generating lower revenue.

Understanding regional trends can support better sales planning, marketing strategies, and resource allocation.

### 📦 3. Monthly Units Sold by Category

**Visualization:** Multi-Series Line Chart

This chart analyzes the number of units sold across different product categories over the six-month period.

**🛍️ Product Categories:**

- 🏋️ Fitness
- 👕 Apparel
- 👟 Footwear
- 🚴 Cycling
- 🏊 Aquatics
- 🎒 Accessories
- ⚽ Sports Equipment

**🔍 Key Insights:**

- 🥇 Fitness reaches approximately **114 units sold in September**, one of the highest category-level monthly sales volumes.
- Fitness also shows strong performance in July, with approximately 100 units sold.
- Apparel reaches approximately 88 units sold in August.
- Footwear experiences fluctuations in monthly sales volume.
- Different categories demonstrate varying demand patterns throughout the reporting period.

**💡 Business Insight:**

Tracking monthly product demand helps businesses identify popular categories, monitor changing purchasing patterns, and make informed inventory decisions.

## 💡 Key Business Findings

### 📈 Revenue Growth

Monthly revenue increased from approximately **$10.8K in May to $18.4K in August**, representing growth of approximately **70.4%**.

This highlights a strong period of sales expansion.

### 🏆 Peak Sales Month

August achieved the highest monthly revenue, making it the strongest month in the reporting period.

### 🌎 Regional Sales Performance

The Northeast demonstrates consistently strong revenue performance, while the West shows a noticeable increase during August.

### 🛍️ Product Category Demand

Fitness stands out as a high-volume product category, particularly during July and September.

### 📉 Sales Decline

Revenue decreased from approximately $18.4K in August to $14.0K in October, a decline of approximately **23.9%**.

Further analysis could help determine the factors contributing to this change.
